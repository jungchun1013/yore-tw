關於 yore.tw

About yore.tw

yore.tw 是一間一個人的小型遊戲工作室，做的是放在瀏覽器裡、不用下載就能玩的像素遊戲。目前有兩款作品：由貓咪經營馬卡龍店的放置遊戲 Meowcaron 喵卡龍，以及關於輪迴的自動戰鬥 RPG Souls in Abyss 輪迴無止盡。

yore.tw is a one-person game studio making small pixel games that run in the browser, with nothing to download. There are two games so far: Meowcaron, an idle game about a macaron bakery run by cats, and Souls in Abyss, an auto-battle RPG about rebirth.

關於我

About me

嗨，我是 Joan。我在台南出生長大，那是一座有很多歷史、文化和好吃東西的城市。

Hi, I am Joan. I was born and raised in Tainan, a city in Taiwan full of history, culture and good food.

我喜歡畫畫和數位繪圖，也喜歡攝影、打羽球、讀小說，還有聽台灣的獨立樂團。做遊戲是把這些喜歡的東西放在一起的方法：畫一隻貓，讓牠在店裡走來走去，再幫牠想一句台詞。

I enjoy drawing and digital art, photography, badminton, reading novels, and listening to Taiwanese indie bands. Making games is how I put these things together: draw a cat, let it wander around a shop, and think of something for it to say.

我想做的是那種可以在通勤時、睡前或休息時打開，玩幾分鐘就讓人心情變好的遊戲。

I want to make games you can open on the bus, before bed or during a break, and feel a little better after a few minutes.

遊戲是怎麼做的

How the games are made

每款遊戲都從一張紙上的想法開始，然後做成可以玩的原型，再一點一點調整。角色和場景都是像素畫，程式用 TypeScript 寫成，Meowcaron 使用 Phaser 遊戲引擎。每次更新都會在電腦和手機上實際玩過，確認字看得清楚、按鈕好按。

Each game starts as an idea on paper, becomes a playable prototype, and then gets tuned little by little. The characters and scenes are pixel art, the code is written in TypeScript, and Meowcaron runs on the Phaser game engine. Every update is played on both a computer and a phone to make sure the text is readable and the buttons are easy to press.

遊戲的節奏也會用模擬程式測試。例如 Meowcaron 的一輪大約是兩天，而且不會因為放著好幾天就突然全部解鎖。

Pacing is checked with simulations as well. For example, one round of Meowcaron takes about two days, and leaving it alone for days does not suddenly unlock everything.

我在乎的事

What I care about

  • 不用小字，手機上也要看得清楚。
  • No tiny text. Everything has to be readable on a phone.
  • 畫面上的東西都要有用途，不放純裝飾。
  • Everything on screen should do something.
  • 放著也會成長，但主動玩會更有感覺。
  • Games grow while you are away, and playing actively still feels rewarding.
  • 廣告永遠不能擋住遊戲。不看廣告也能玩完整個遊戲。
  • Ads never get in the way. You can play the whole game without watching one.
