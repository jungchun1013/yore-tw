這裡有一個人，還有一隻貓

One person, and a cat

喵，又見面了。首頁那隻等你摸摸的貓就是我。這個地方叫 yore.tw，遊戲是 Joan 做的，我負責歡迎你。

Hello again. I’m the cat waiting for a pat on the home page. This place is yore.tw. Joan makes the games, and I welcome people in.

Joan 和她的小世界

Joan’s little worlds

Joan 來自台南，喜歡畫畫。在這裡，一隻貓不只是一張圖：你可以摸摸牠，看牠在店裡走動，轉頭又發現牠窩在烤箱旁睡著了。畫裡的小傢伙有了自己的生活，你也能加入。這是這些小遊戲最想留給你的樂趣。

Joan is from Tainan, Taiwan, and loves drawing. Here, a cat can be more than a picture: you can pet it, watch it wander through a shop, then find it curled up beside the oven. The little creatures have lives of their own, and you get to join them. That’s much of the fun of these games.

像喵卡龍，開店當然要做點心、招呼客人，但也有很多時間可以看看貓咪在忙什麼。一張坐墊、一間貓屋，就能讓小店多出一些值得停下來看的事。

In Meowcaron, there are treats to bake and customers to serve, but there’s time to watch the cats, too. A cushion or a cat house gives you another small reason to stop and look.

留一點時間給遊戲

A little time to play

Joan 想做的是休息時可以打開的小遊戲。不必一次玩很久，今天認識一隻貓，明天替冒險者找到一件合適的裝備，也算有了一段自己的故事。

Joan wants to make small games you can open during a break. You don’t need a long session. Meet a cat today, find your adventurer a better weapon tomorrow, and a story of your own starts to take shape.

輪迴無止盡走的是另一條路。那裡沒有暖烤箱，只有越走越深的旅程；但你不會空手重新開始，走過的路會留下一點力量，同行的夥伴也會留下來。下一次醒來，又能往前走一段。

Souls in Abyss follows a different road. There’s no warm oven there, just a journey that leads deeper. Still, you never begin again empty-handed: some strength remains, and your companions stay with you. When you wake again, you can go a little further.

想說的話，歡迎留下來

Leave us a note

喜歡哪隻貓，在哪一段冒險卡住，或只是想打聲招呼，都可以寫信給 Joan。這裡是她一個人慢慢做的小地方，很高興你找到我們。

Tell Joan which cat you like, where your adventure got stuck, or just say hello. You can write to her here. This is a small place she’s making on her own. We’re glad you found us.
