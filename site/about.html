About yore.tw

yore.tw 是一個人的小工作室，做放在瀏覽器裡、不用下載就能玩的像素遊戲。第一款作品是 Meowcaron 喵卡龍：一間由貓咪經營的馬卡龍店，拍拍店長、雇貓、升級產線、看貓咪在店裡過日子。

yore.tw is a one-person studio making small pixel games that run in the browser. The first one is Meowcaron: a macaron bakery run by cats. Pat the owner, hire cats, upgrade the line, and watch the cats live their lives in the shop.

我們在乎的事

What we care about

  • 不用小字：手機上也要看得清楚。
  • No tiny text: it has to read on a phone.
  • 每個畫面上的東西都有功能，沒有純裝飾。
  • Everything on screen does something; no pure decoration.
  • 放著也能長大，但主動玩會有感。
  • It grows while you are away, and playing actively is noticeably rewarding.
  • 獎勵廣告永遠是選擇性的，不看也能玩完整遊戲。
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; the whole game is playable without them.
