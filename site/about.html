關於 yore game studio

About yore game studio

yore game studio 是來自台灣的一人遊戲工作室，主打經營養成與放置類遊戲。精緻像素 + 幻彩美術風格設計。

yore game studio is a one-person game studio from Taiwan, focused on management and idle games, with detailed pixel art in iridescent colours.

關於 Joan

About Joan

Joan 來自台南，平常畫畫、攝影、打羽球、看小說，聽台灣的獨立樂團。遊戲裡的貓、點心和風景，大多從她的圖開始。

Joan is from Tainan, Taiwan. She draws, takes photos, plays badminton, reads novels and listens to Taiwanese indie bands. Most of the cats, treats and scenery in the games started as her drawings.

喵卡龍是一間由貓經營的馬卡龍店。做點心、招呼客人，也可以什麼都不做，看貓在店裡走來走去。

Meowcaron is a macaron shop run by cats. Bake, serve customers, or do nothing and watch the cats wander around.

遊戲的方向

What the games are for

這些遊戲是給休息時間玩的。一次幾分鐘就好，關掉之後進度會留著，下次打開接著玩。

These games are made for breaks. A few minutes at a time is enough. Progress stays when you close the tab, and you pick up where you left off.

奈落魂迴是放置型的自動戰鬥 RPG。角色會老去、會死，然後帶著上一世的技能和夥伴重新開始，一次比一次走得更深。

Souls in Abyss is an idle auto-battle RPG. Your character ages, dies, and starts over with the skills and companions of the last life, going a little deeper each time.
