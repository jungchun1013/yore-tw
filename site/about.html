About yore game studio

yore game studio 是來自台灣的一人遊戲工作室，主打經營養成與放置類遊戲。精緻像素 + 幻彩美術風格設計。

yore game studio is a one-person game studio from Taiwan, focused on management and idle games, with detailed pixel art in iridescent colours.

關於 Joan

About Joan

Joan 來自台灣，台南囡仔。前舞台燈光設計師和羽球選手，副業是 AI 研究員，喜歡畫畫、攝影、打羽球、閱讀小說，聽團，喜歡波士頓晚霞的色彩。遊戲靈感來自生活。

Joan is from Taiwan, a Tainan kid. A former stage lighting designer and badminton player, with AI research on the side. She likes drawing, photography, badminton, novels, live music, and the colours of a Boston sunset. The games take their inspiration from everyday life.
