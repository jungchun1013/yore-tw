回報問題時

When reporting a bug

如果方便的話，請附上這些資訊，我會比較快找到原因：

If you can, please include the following. It helps me find the cause faster:

  • 哪一款遊戲
  • Which game
  • 使用的裝置和瀏覽器，例如 iPhone 的 Safari
  • Your device and browser, for example Safari on an iPhone
  • 發生了什麼事，以及你當時在做什麼
  • What happened and what you were doing at the time
  • 如果有的話，附上一張截圖
  • A screenshot, if you have one

關於存檔

About saves

遊戲存檔只存在你自己的瀏覽器裡，我看不到，也沒辦法幫你找回。清除瀏覽器資料或換一台裝置時，進度就不會跟著過去。

Game saves live only in your own browser. I cannot see them or recover them for you. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, your progress does not come along.
