If something went wrong

告訴她是哪款遊戲、發生了什麼事，以及當時用的是手機還是電腦。有截圖的話，也可以一起附上，會更容易找到問題。

Let her know which game you were playing, what happened, and whether you were on a phone or a computer. A screenshot helps, too, if you have one.

小提醒：進度存在你遊玩的瀏覽器裡，換裝置或清除瀏覽器資料前要留意，Joan 沒辦法替你找回刪掉的存檔。

One small reminder: your progress stays in the browser you play in. Take care before clearing its data or switching devices; Joan can’t recover a deleted save.
