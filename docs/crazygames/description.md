## English

**Title:** Meowcaron

**Tagline (one line):** A macaron bakery run by cats. Pat, hire, upgrade, and let it grow while you are away.

**Short description (≤ 300 chars):**
Run a cosy pixel macaron bakery with a staff of cats. Pat the owner to bake, hire eleven kinds of cats, upgrade a real macaron production line, furnish the shop and keep the cats from slacking. Progress continues offline. Free, no account, in English and Traditional Chinese.

**Long description:**
Meowcaron is an idle clicker set in a small macaron bakery staffed entirely by cats.

Pat the owner cat and a macaron pops out of the oven. Steady tapping builds up heat that lifts the whole shop's output for a while. Hire kittens, apprentices, bakers, couriers and more: each cat type produces on its own, has its own journal page, and reacts in its own way when you pet it on the floor.

The kitchen is a real production line. Energy from pats and working cats gathers in the mixer, batter runs past the piping station, bakes in the oven, gets wrapped at the packaging machine and drops into the sack. Every upgrade is a real step of making macarons: sifting almond flour, whipping meringue, resting the shells, convection baking, ganache filling, gift boxes, a ripening fridge. The machines on the counter change as you buy them.

Furnish the shop with cushions, a cat tree, a cat house, a tent, a scratching post and a hanging ball. Cats nap in them, climb, scratch and swat. They also slack off: napping, cuddling, squabbling or staring out of the window lowers production until you tap them back to work.

Late-game cats bring abilities you trigger by petting them when they glow: the Influencer Cat posts photos that bring in crowds of customers, the Cat Deity blesses the shop, the Time Cat speeds everything up, the Cosmic Cat rains stardust that turns into permanent stars.

Open a new shop when you are ready: you keep stars that boost production forever. The bakery keeps baking while you are away, and you can double offline earnings or start a Catnip rush by watching an optional ad. Ads never block progress.

Features
- Idle production with offline earnings (up to 8 hours, extendable)
- 11 cat types with journals, pet reactions and three special-ability cats
- Process-based kitchen upgrades that change the machines on screen
- Furniture, toys and a slacking system you manage by tapping
- Patting heat: active play stays rewarding all game long
- Prestige with permanent stars and a star shop
- Customer codex and a shop photo feed
- Traditional Chinese and English, large readable text, phone and desktop layouts
- Optional rewarded ads only

**Controls:**
Click or tap the owner cat to bake. Tap cats on the floor to pet them (and to stop them slacking). Buy cats, upgrades and furniture in the side panel. Everything works with mouse or touch; no keyboard needed.

**Tags:** idle, clicker, incremental, cats, cozy, pixel art, management, bakery

**Category:** Idle / Clicker

## 繁體中文

**名稱：** Meowcaron 喵卡龍

**一句話：** 貓咪經營的馬卡龍店。拍拍、雇貓、升級，放著也會長大。

**簡介（300 字內）：**
一間全部由貓咪經營的像素馬卡龍店。拍店長烤馬卡龍，雇用十一種貓咪員工，升級真正的馬卡龍製程，佈置家具，還要把偷懶的貓叫回來工作。離線也會持續生產。免費、不用帳號，支援繁體中文與英文。

**完整介紹：**
Meowcaron 是一款放置點擊遊戲，背景是一間小小的馬卡龍店，員工全是貓。

拍一下店長貓，烤箱就吐出一顆馬卡龍；連續拍會累積熱度，讓整間店的產量提高一陣子。雇用小奶貓、學徒貓、烘焙貓、外送貓等等，每一種貓都會自己生產，有自己的日誌頁面，在地板上被摸的時候也各有反應。

廚房是真正的產線：拍拍與工作中的貓產生的能量匯進攪拌機，麵糊經過擠花台、進烤箱烤成馬卡龍、到包裝機變成禮物、最後掉進袋子。每一個升級都是真實的馬卡龍工序：杏仁粉過篩、打蛋白霜、晾皮、旋風烘烤、甘納許夾餡、禮盒分格、熟成冷藏。檯面上的機器會隨著購買一階一階改變。

幫店裡添購坐墊、貓跳台、貓屋、帳篷、貓抓板和懸吊球球。貓會去睡覺、爬跳台、抓抓、揮打。牠們也會偷懶：睡覺、貼貼、吵架、看窗外都會降低產量，點牠一下就回去工作。

後期的貓有可以手動觸發的技能，發光時摸牠：網紅貓發文帶來一群客人、貓貓神給全店加護、時間貓讓一切加速、宇宙貓下一場星塵雨，星塵集滿變成永久星星。

準備好了就開新店：星星會保留，永久提升產量。離開時店還在烤；回來可以選擇看一段廣告把離線收益翻倍，或啟動貓草加速。廣告永遠不會擋住進度。

特色
- 放置生產與離線收益（最多 8 小時，可延長）
- 11 種貓咪，各有日誌、被摸反應，後期三種特殊技能貓
- 依馬卡龍製程設計的廚具升級，畫面上的機器會跟著變
- 家具、玩具與偷懶系統，點一下管理
- 拍拍熱度：主動玩全程都有感
- 開新店（轉生）與永久星星、星星商店
- 客人圖鑑與店家照片牆
- 繁體中文與英文、大字清楚、手機與電腦版面
- 只有選擇性的獎勵廣告

**操作：**
點擊或輕觸店長貓烤馬卡龍。點地板上的貓可以摸牠（也能叫偷懶的貓回去工作）。在側邊面板購買貓咪、升級和家具。滑鼠或觸控都可以，不需要鍵盤。
